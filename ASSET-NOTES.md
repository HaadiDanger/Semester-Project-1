# Asset notes

## Current application

- Profiles without a permitted uploaded photo, including every seeded sample, use initials. Hero and spotlight cards use the same fallback on code-drawn gradients in `src/index.css`; genuine permitted photos can appear there too.
- All eleven fictional stock/AI portraits were removed from `public/images/`. No stock or AI face is assigned to a development identity.
- Genuine account owners can upload their own raster images. These are stored in private Supabase buckets after validation and metadata stripping, and are streamed only when the viewer is authorized. They are not repository assets.
- `public/images/profile-fallback.svg` is a code-drawn UI asset from the redesign. The IUST Orbit favicon in `public/favicon.svg` is original code-drawn orbital artwork, updated on 6 November 2026. The header/footer use the `Orbit` icon from the existing ISC-licensed `lucide-react` dependency.
- Development price evidence is generated by `scripts/fixtures/sample-evidence.mjs`; it is synthetic raster artwork, not a receipt or proof of a genuine price.
- Outfit and Fraunces fonts are bundled in `public/fonts/`, with the accompanying `outfit-OFL.txt` and `fraunces-OFL.txt` licenses. `src/fonts.css` provides local font loading and system fallbacks.

## Campus café food photographs

The **30 photographs** in `public/images/menu/` were retrieved from Wikimedia Commons on **6 November 2026** and visually reviewed. They are real food photographs, matched to the menu names or preparation styles and labelled **illustrative**. They do not depict NESCAFÉ IUST’s actual servings. The cappuccino photograph also supplies the café cover.

The local copies total **3,284,052 bytes**. Transformations: thumbnail resizing where supplied by Wikimedia, auto-orientation, metadata stripping and WebP conversion; the application crops images to fit their cards. These copies and adaptations retain the licences listed below, including the same share-alike licence for CC BY-SA adaptations. The UI links each image to its author/source and licence and describes the changes.

[`scripts/data/menu-photos.json`](scripts/data/menu-photos.json) records the complete metadata, original/download URLs, source dates, original SHA-1, downloaded SHA-256, final SHA-256, dimensions and byte sizes. [`scripts/data/menu-photo-sources.json`](scripts/data/menu-photo-sources.json) records the curated source choices. See [the photograph workflow](docs/cafes.md#photograph-provenance-and-updates) for preparation and replacements.
